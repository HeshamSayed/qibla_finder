# Privacy Policy - Qiblati App

**Last Updated:** November 10, 2025

## Introduction

Qiblati ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Qiblati mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- **Purpose:** Calculate accurate Qibla direction and prayer times
- **What we collect:** GPS coordinates (precise and approximate location)
- **How we use it:** Processed locally on your device, NOT stored on our servers
- **Third-party sharing:** Location coordinates sent to geocoding services only to display city/country names

### Device Information
- **Purpose:** App functionality and advertising
- **What we collect:** Device model, operating system, compass sensor data, device identifiers
- **How we use it:** For app operation and advertising purposes

## How We Use Your Information

- Calculate accurate Qibla direction to Makkah
- Display accurate prayer times based on your location
- Send prayer time reminder notifications
- Display your city and country name
- Show advertisements through Google AdMob

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob
We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:
- Device advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA)
- IP address
- Approximate location
- Ad interaction data

**AdMob Privacy Policy:** https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Opt-out:** You can opt out of personalized ads in Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

**Ad-Free Option:** Watch a video ad to remove all ads for 24 hours

## Data Security

- Location processing happens locally on your device
- HTTPS used for all network communications
- We do NOT store personal data on our servers
- We do NOT create user accounts or profiles

## Children's Privacy

Our app does NOT knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

- We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13
- If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately
- We use family-friendly ad settings through Google AdMob

## Your Rights

- **Access:** Request information about data we've collected
- **Deletion:** Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data
- **Opt-Out:** Disable location permissions or opt out of personalized ads in device settings

## Permissions Explained

- **Location (Fine & Coarse):** For Qibla direction and prayer times
- **Internet:** For ads and location name lookup
- **Notifications:** For prayer time reminders
- **Exact Alarms:** For precise prayer notifications
- **Vibrate:** For haptic feedback when facing Qibla
- **Boot Completed:** To reschedule notifications after device restart

## Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data with:

- **Google AdMob:** For advertising purposes
- **Geocoding Services:** To convert coordinates to location names
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or court order

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date and posting the new policy in the app.
